Your old device, their new start

We collect, restore, and redistribute electronics to students and families who need them most.

How it works

Simple, transparent, impactful

Every donated device goes through a thoughtful process designed to protect privacy, reduce waste, and help students succeed.

01

You donate

Drop off or ship any working device, we handle logistics and make the process easy from start to finish.

02

We inspect

Each device goes through diagnostics, cleaning, and a certified data wipe to keep personal information secure.

03

We restore

Devices are repaired, upgraded, and optimized with modern software to extend their lifespan.

04

We deliver

Restored devices are matched with local students and families, at absolutely no cost.

Why we exist

Closing the digital divide

Millions of functional devices are discarded each year while students struggle without access. We're here to rewrite that story.

↓46%

e-waste reduction

Refurbishing a single laptop saves 190kg CO₂ and prevents toxic landfill leakage.

+2.3M

students without devices

In Massachusetts alone, thousands lack home internet or a computer. We bridge that gap.

Our pledge

Technology should be accessible

Every device we restore is provided completely free to recipients. We focus on transparency, responsible refurbishing, and making sure each device reaches someone who truly needs it.
